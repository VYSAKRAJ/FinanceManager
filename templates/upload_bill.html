Expected CSV Format

Your CSV should have headers. Supported column names:

ColumnAccepted Names
DateDate, date, Transaction Date, transaction_date
MerchantMerchant, merchant, Description, description
AmountAmount, amount, Debit, debit
CategoryCategory, category (optional)

Supported date formats:

  • YYYY-MM-DD (e.g. 2024-03-15)
  • DD/MM/YYYY (e.g. 15/03/2024)
  • MM/DD/YYYY (e.g. 03/15/2024)
  • DD-MM-YYYY (e.g. 15-03-2024)
  • DD Mon YYYY (e.g. 15 Mar 2024)
